# Privacy policy

Quikscript Reader does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Web pages.** When you switch a page to Quikscript, the extension reads its text inside your browser to redraw it. Nothing leaves your computer.
- **Settings.** Your settings (style, size, hover options, translation language) are saved with `chrome.storage`, which Chrome may sync between your own devices if you use Chrome sync.
- **Translations.** Words are translated by Chrome's built-in, on-device translator. Translations are cached on your computer with `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Dictation.** The dictation page uses Chrome's speech recognition (the Web Speech API). Depending on your Chrome version and settings, Chrome may send the audio to Google's servers to recognise it, under Google's own privacy policy. The extension itself does not record, store or send any audio.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no remote code.** The extension makes no network requests of its own.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
