# Third-party notices

Quikscript Reader bundles data derived from the following works.

## Abbots Morton Experiment

Letter outlines in `data/qs-data.json`, by Nathan Galt, under the Apache License 2.0
(see `Abbots-Morton-Experiment-Apache-2.0.txt`). https://github.com/adiabatic/abbots-morton-experiment

Changes: the outlines were extracted from the font; half letters were clipped from full ones;
ten Senior ligatures (They, Way, Why, Vie, See, Jay and J'ai + Utter, Out + Tea, Day + Eat, See + Eat)
were drawn from its outlines and strokes of the same weight; pen paths (`data/pen-paths.json`)
were computed from the outlines.

## Abbots Morton Spaceport

Joining rules in `data/qs-data.json`, derived from the glyph data of Abbots Morton Spaceport
by Nathan Galt, under the MIT License (see `Abbots-Morton-Spaceport-MIT.md`).
https://github.com/adiabatic/abbots-morton-spaceport

## CMU Pronouncing Dictionary

Pronunciations in `data/dict.txt.gz` and `data/ipa.txt.gz`, derived from the CMU Pronouncing
Dictionary, Copyright (C) 1993-2015 Carnegie Mellon University (see `CMU-Pronouncing-Dictionary.txt`).

## NRL letter-to-sound rules

`data/nrl.json`: the Naval Research Laboratory text-to-phoneme rules (Elovitz et al., 1976), public domain.

Quikscript was devised by Kingsley Read (1887-1975).
